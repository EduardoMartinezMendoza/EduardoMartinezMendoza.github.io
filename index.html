01 ABOUT

About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at the University of North Texas, working with John Krueger. My research focuses on set theory and infinite combinatorics, particularly trees, linear orders, forcing, and independence results. My dissertation is titled Combinatorial and Independence Results Concerning Aronszajn Trees. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in May 2027 and received a B.S. in Applied Mathematics from the Instituto Tecnológico Autónomo de México (ITAM) in 2019.
